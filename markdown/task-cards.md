# Task Cards — Free ADHD Printable

Part of the Free ADHD Printables Pack by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.
Fillable on screen, printable on paper. No account needed.

## How to use task cards

1. Pick **one** thing that feels heavy.
2. Write **one tiny step** on a card — smaller than you think you need.
3. Do that step. Check the box. That counts.
4. If you still have energy, pick another card. If not, rest. Both are wins.

On low-energy days, "brush my teeth" or "open the laptop" is a perfectly good card.

## Open the fillable version

Open `printables/task-cards.html` in your browser, type into the fields, then print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).
Screen buttons and headers are hidden when printed.
