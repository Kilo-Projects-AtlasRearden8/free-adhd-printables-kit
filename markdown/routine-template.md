Fillable on screen, printable on paper. No account needed.

## How to use routine templates

1. Pick **one** routine to start with — morning, evening, or reset.
2. Keep each step tiny. You can skip anything. Rest is part of the routine.
3. Check off what you did. What you didn't do can wait.

There's no "right way" to do a routine. The routine works for you, not the other way around.

## Routines included

### Morning routine
Gentle start. Do what you can, in any order.
- Wake up
- Drink water
- Wash face / brush teeth
- Get dressed
- One small thing I'm looking forward to
- My own step (blank)

### Evening routine
Wind down. Nothing here is mandatory.
- Put dishes away (or leave them)
- Set out tomorrow's clothes
- Brush teeth / wash face
- One thing that went well today
- Lights out when I'm ready
- My own step (blank)

### Reset routine (low-energy days)
For when everything feels like too much. Pick one, or pick none.
- Drink a glass of water
- Sit by a window for two minutes
- Stretch once
- Text one person "thinking of you"
- Rest without guilt
- My own step (blank)
