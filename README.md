# Free ADHD Printables Pack

Fillable, printable task cards, routine templates, and an energy tracker for low-energy and ADHD days.
Kind, small-steps design. No account needed. Free to print and share.

Made by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026).

## What's inside

| Printable | What it's for |
|-----------|---------------|
| [Task cards](printables/task-cards.html) | Break anything into tiny steps. One card, one small thing, one win. |
| [Routine templates](printables/routine-template.html) | Gentle morning, evening, and reset routines with room to rest. |
| [Energy tracker](printables/energy-tracker.html) | Rate your energy day by day, notice what drains you, and plan to match. |

Each printable is:
- **Fillable** — type into fields on screen, then print.
- **Printable** — print buttons and headers hide automatically when you print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).
- **Free** — no account, no signup, no tracking.

## How to use

1. Open any printable in your browser.
2. Click or tap the fields and type your answers.
3. Print or save as PDF.
4. On a bad day, do one thing on one card. That counts.
