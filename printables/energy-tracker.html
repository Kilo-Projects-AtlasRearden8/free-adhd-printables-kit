How to use the energy tracker

  1. At the end of each day, rate your energy from 1 (very low) to 5 (very high).
  2. Jot down what helped and what drained you.
  3. After a week, look for patterns. Plan hard things on higher-energy days.

A low-energy day isn't a failure. It's information. Be kind to yourself.
